# Vibes 0.6.1

A quick fix for friends not showing up.

- Fixed: with Weather turned on, the app could get stuck waiting on the location permission and show an empty sky — your friends are always safe on the server, but they might not have appeared until later. Weather now loads quietly in the background and never holds up your friends list.
