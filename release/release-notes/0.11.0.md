# Vibes 0.11.0

Put your presence sky on the desktop.

- New widget mode: turn it on from the menu bar icon and Vibes lives directly on your desktop as a transparent, chrome-free sky that sits behind your other windows and stays put as you switch spaces.
- Drag anywhere on the sky to move it, and Vibes remembers where you left it — including across restarts.
- Bring the full window back any time from the menu bar icon, the Dock menu, or by clicking the Dock icon.
- Fixes an issue where an unreadable settings file could be overwritten instead of backed up.
