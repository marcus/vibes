# Vibes 0.5.0

Your friends are now in orbit.

- New orbit view: everyone online floats as a glowing orb — bigger orbs mean bigger coding days, and the repos they're working in hang alongside as little moons.
- The ring around each orb tracks their day against their own typical pace: green for lines added, red for lines removed, and a gold badge when someone laps their usual day.
- Prefer the classic view? A toggle in the header switches between orbit and the card list, and Vibes remembers your choice.
- Slimmer header with a live "in orbit" count, and a one-dot presence light — click it to go online or offline.
- Offline friends drift along the bottom with a quiet summary of what they last shipped.
