# Vibes 0.10.5

Makes the ambient Orbit dramatically more energy-efficient while keeping its calm motion and familiar appearance.

- Orbit motion now runs through Core Animation instead of rebuilding the full sky many times per second.
- Motion still pauses for Reduce Motion and when the window is hidden, minimized, or fully covered.
- The center pulse keeps its original footprint, and the drifting dock now has more balanced spacing.
