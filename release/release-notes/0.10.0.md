# Vibes 0.10.0

See the whole community's pulse.

- A new network pulse shows the coding happening across everyone on Vibes right now — total lines added and removed today, and how many people are vibing. In orbit view it's a glowing core at the center of your sky; in list view it's a 14-day history up top, so you can see which days the community is busiest.
- Fully anonymous and aggregate: no names, projects, or per-person numbers, and nothing shows until at least three people have committed.
