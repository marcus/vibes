# Vibes 0.6.0

Music and weather come to the sky.

- New: share what you're playing. Turn on Music in Settings → Sharing and Vibes shows the track from Spotify or Apple Music on your orb and card — hover an orb to see the full title. It clears when you pause, and nothing is shared unless you turn it on.
- New: local weather. Turn on Weather to show your conditions and temperature next to your name. Use your current location or just type a city. Your location never leaves your Mac — friends only see the weather, and your city name stays private unless you opt in.
- New setting: hide the Dock icon and run Vibes from the menu bar.
- Clicking an invite link now always lands in the copy of Vibes you already have open.
