# Vibes 0.5.1

Vibes now follows you to your next Mac.

- One-click setup on a new Mac: if you use iCloud Keychain, Vibes finds your account and offers "Use this Mac as @you" right on the setup screen.
- Or link with a code: Settings → General → Link Another Mac creates a short one-time code to type into the new Mac. You can also copy it as a link to message to yourself.
- New Devices list in Settings shows every Mac on your account — with one-click removal for ones you no longer use.
- Every Mac gets its own credential, so removing one never signs out the others.
