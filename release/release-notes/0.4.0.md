# Vibes 0.4.0

A redesigned home view that makes it easy to see who's around at a glance.

- New presence-grouped layout: friends who are coding right now show as full cards, while everyone else collapses into a quiet "Away" list.
- Refreshed Aurora II look — warmer cards, a softer palette, and a breathing ring around the avatar of anyone who's online.
- Your synced friend activity now stays put when you go offline, instead of clearing.
