# Vibes 0.10.4

Uses less energy when Vibes is in the background and makes its privacy boundaries clearer.

- The orbit now pauses when its window is hidden, minimized, or fully covered, avoiding unnecessary background work.
- Ambient orbit motion shares a lighter rendering clock while preserving the same calm movement.
- The privacy page now explains more precisely what Vibes shares and what always stays on your Mac.
