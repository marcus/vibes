# Vibes 0.5.5

Joining from an invite is now one step.

- New: if you downloaded Vibes from an invite link, setup spots your invite automatically — enter your name and you're connected to the friend who invited you. No more going back to the invite page.
- The setup screen now shows who invited you before you sign up.
