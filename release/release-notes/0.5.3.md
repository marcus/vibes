# Vibes 0.5.3

Small touches that make Vibes feel more at home on your Mac.

- Buttons like Copy Link now confirm with a little pop and a checkmark, so you know it worked.
- New setting: open Vibes automatically when you log in (off unless you turn it on).
- New setting: choose Light, Dark, or Follow System for the app's look.
- Invite links you share now show a proper card with the Vibes logo in Messages and chat apps.
- The invite page friends land on got a full redesign — it now matches the website and makes downloading obvious.
